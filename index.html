Connect to your TV

Your TV must be on and on the same Wi-Fi network as this device.

Find it in your router's app (look for "Living Room TV" or similar), or on the TV under Network → Network Information.

Let this browser talk to your TV

Your TV uses a security certificate that browsers don't recognize. You only need to approve it once:

  1. Tap Open TV link. A warning page opens in a new tab.
  2. Choose Advanced, then Proceed (or Show Details → visit this website on iPhone).
  3. You'll land on a plain page that says Unhandled Exception. That's normal: it means the TV answered. Close that tab and tap Retry here.
Open TV link

If your browser asks to access devices on your local network, choose Allow.

"This site can't be reached"? Then this device can't see the TV at all. Check that the TV is on, that this device is on the same Wi-Fi as the TV (not mobile data or a guest network), and that any VPN is off. On iPhone, also check Settings → your browser → Local Network is on. On Android, check Settings → Apps → your browser → Permissions → Nearby devices (or Local network) is allowed.
